# Leeds release audit · 25 September 2026

This audit applies to the local `leeds-local-elections-v0.1.0` development package. It does **not** authorize publication of that package. Its `publication_allowed` flag remains false. A released version must be built separately, with its own manifest, checksums and rollback copy.

| Component | Evidence found | Release decision |
|---|---|---|
| Scheduled results, 2021–2024 | The source notebooks name the House of Commons Library Local Elections Handbook workbooks. The [2021](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/data/parliament-elections-data/2021-local-elections-handbook-and-dataset/), [2022](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/data/parliament-elections-data/2022-local-elections-handbook-and-dataset/), [2023](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/2023-local-elections-handbook-and-dataset/) and [2024](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/2024-local-elections-handbook-and-dataset/) pages expose downloads with the same filenames as the four local raw workbooks. The Commons Library [permits reuse of its published data under the Open Parliament Licence](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/data/). The earlier isolated rebuild reproduced the transformed result tables from the local files. | Upstream publication and licence identified. Exact byte comparison with a fresh upstream download remains outstanding; do not describe the local workbooks as hash-verified against the website. Add the required Parliamentary attribution to the release. |
| 2026 council results; Morley South 2025 | The [Leeds City Council archive](https://datamillnorth.org/dataset/local-election-results-20jwj) lists both official files under OGL v3. The 2026 candidate records were checked against the council results page; the Morley South declaration was checked from the rendered PDF. | Source and licence are recorded in `sources.json`; retain direct links and attribution. |
| Farnley & Wortley, 10 October 2024 | The archive PDF has zeroes for all candidate votes and no winner. The package rejected it. | Keep as an explicitly missing event. A secondary report cannot silently replace a declaration. |
| 2025 ward map | The ONS [BFC V2 product](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/612b175b-987c-4acf-8ca4-1f93c6947928/wards-may-2025-boundaries-uk-bfc-v2) has the same internal layer name as the local GeoPackage, despite its BGC filename. An official ArcGIS query returned 33 Leeds features with exactly the package's 33 ward codes. All compared shapes were valid; maximum Hausdorff distance was 0.0000183 degrees and maximum symmetric-difference area was 0.374% of an official shape. [ONS boundary guidance](https://www.ons.gov.uk/methodology/geography/geographicalproducts/digitalboundaries) states the OGL and required ONS/OS credits. | Strong source match, but not an exact geometry match. Rebuild the web layer from the official BFC V2 service for a new release and include both required credits. Continue labelling all displayed years as *2025 boundaries*. |
| Census population reference | The local OA-to-ward lookup and ward atlas have hashes and reproduced aggregates, but exact upstream files/licence links are not recorded. | Exclude this layer from the first hosted factual release, or rebuild it from linked official ONS sources. |
| By-election coverage | The council archive lists Farnley 2024 and Morley South 2025, but no exhaustive register was certified. | Describe the exact included event set. Do not claim a complete latest-results history or current councillor composition. |
| Forecast | The first time-ordered backtest is in `forecast-research/`. Its 2026 holdout is materially worse than earlier folds. | Research only; no prospective ward, seat or council-control projection published. |
