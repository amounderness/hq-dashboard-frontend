# Yorkshire 2026 official-source audit — Sheffield and Barnsley

**State, 5 October 2026:** Read-only source review. The local rc5 package, live Leeds release and v2 feature flags were not changed. This is a focused check of two councils, **not** an exhaustive Yorkshire event register.

## Sheffield City Council

The council's [7 May 2026 ward results](https://www.sheffield.gov.uk/your-city-council/elections/local-elections-results-2026) were saved in ignored research storage as `data/explorer-v2/raw/sheffield-2026-ordinary/official-results-2026.html` (176,839 bytes; SHA-256 `f16198bbee496174699561ee1daedf0ca1abdd5ba2c90072611c0755e3ca48ce`). A read-only [repeatable audit](../tools/explorer_v2/audit_sheffield_2026_ordinary.py) compares that exact snapshot with the rc5 working store.

**Result:** all 28 official wards match 28 ordinary-election contests in rc5. All 196 candidate vote values and elected flags match, as do the 28 electorates and the number of winners per ward. Four staged names are shortened compared with the council wording: Birley `David Cronshaw`/`David James Cronshaw`; Firth Park `Graeme Waddicar`/`Graeme Boyd Waddicar`; Manor Castle `Ruth Abbey`/`Margaret Ruth Abbey` and `Pete Reilly`/`Peter Joseph Reilly`. These are name differences, not vote discrepancies. The council confirms [Beighton and Firth Park elected two councillors each](https://www.sheffield.gov.uk/news/2026/local-elections-2026-statement-persons-nominated); rc5 records two seats in both wards.

The official page states a turnout percentage for **all 28 wards**, while rc5 has no turnout for any of those ordinary-election contests. For the 26 one-seat wards, `candidate votes + rejected ballots` divided by electorate agrees with the percentage to its printed precision in 22 wards. Four differ slightly: Gleadless Valley by 0.012 percentage points, Graves Park by 0.005, Richmond by 0.006 and Southey by 0.010. Use the council's stated rate with a field note rather than silently treating the calculation as exact. In two-seat Beighton and Firth Park, candidate votes can exceed the number of participating voters; do not use a single-seat ballot formula. The council page's “total ballot papers issued” wording is not consistently equivalent to candidate votes plus rejected ballots. Keep the reported turnout and its source distinct from an inferred ballot count. Council declarations or a direct clarification would strengthen the two-seat field review before a wider release.

The current [election-notices page](https://www.sheffield.gov.uk/your-city-council/elections/election-notices) lists the Southey and Walkley 2026 by-elections now in rc5. Its visible notices align with those two events, but the page alone does not certify that the full 2026 event register is exhaustive. The [aggregate results index](https://www.sheffield.gov.uk/your-city-council/elections/city-council-parish-council-election-results) still gives Andrew Telford 258 votes in Walkley, while the [individual council result](https://www.sheffield.gov.uk/your-city-council/elections/walkley-by-election-results) and signed declaration give 541; rc5 uses the declaration and explains the difference.

## Barnsley Council

The council's [Penistone East by-election result](https://www.barnsley.gov.uk/services/voting-and-elections/election-results/penistone-east-ward-by-election-20-august-2026/) confirms a **20 August 2026** principal-council poll. John Roberts (Labour) was elected from eight candidates, with 1,728 votes. The candidates total 3,346 votes; the page also reports an electorate of 8,674, seven rejected ballots and 38.58% turnout, calling 3,346 “votes cast.” rc5 has Barnsley's May 2026 three-seat ordinary contest for Penistone East under canonical ward `E05016245`, but **no August by-election**.

The official page was saved as `data/explorer-v2/raw/barnsley-2026-byelections/penistone-east-result.html` (54,501 bytes; SHA-256 `84a8c7e296079a64fd000e0620e87c13480adbfeba2c84b0c024eacfbee6a032`). The [versioned transcription](../tools/explorer_v2/official_byelections_2026_barnsley_review.json) and [read-only checker](../tools/explorer_v2/verify_2026_barnsley_source.py) pass against the snapshot and canonical store. The eight candidate votes sum to 3,346, and `3,346 / 8,674` rounds to the council's 38.58%. **Do not infer ballots issued**: adding the seven rejected papers gives 3,353, whose turnout would round differently. The council page may be using “votes cast” for valid votes, but only a returning-officer declaration or council clarification can settle the paper count. The fixture keeps `ballots_issued` unknown while preserving the stated turnout and rejected figure.
