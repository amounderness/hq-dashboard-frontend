# Leeds source-issue register · 26 September 2026

**Historical v0.5.0 record.** The four vote-source decisions and the defined council-archive event reconciliation were closed in [the v0.6.0 source decision record](leeds-source-decisions-v0.6.0-2026-09-26.md). The blank Farnley council declaration and approximate Morley turnout remain labelled source limitations.

This register records the source decision for staged `leeds-pulse-v0.5.0`. Secondary figures are included only with visible caveats. The original official files remain unchanged.

| Issue | Current evidence and decision | Closure evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Farnley & Wortley by-election, 10 October 2024 | The [council-hosted PDF](https://datamillnorth.org/download/20jwj/7f3/Farnley%20%26%20Wortley%20ward%20by-election%20-%2010%20October%202024.pdf) has zero votes for every candidate and no winner. [West Leeds Dispatch](https://westleedsdispatch.com/farnley-wortley-by-election-result-green-partys-david-blackburn-retakes-seat/) reports all seven candidate votes (3,743 in total), David Blackburn's win and 20.15% turnout. The [secondary election summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Leeds_City_Council_election#Changes_between_2024_and_2026) agrees on candidate votes and rounds turnout to 20.2%. v0.5.0 includes these as **secondary-source** figures, labelled in the contest, history, SDP row and reports. It does not infer electorate or rejected-ballot totals. | Seek a corrected declaration or written confirmation from Leeds electoral services; keep the secondary label until then. |
| Morley South by-election, 12 June 2025 | The [council declaration](https://datamillnorth.org/download/20jwj/bbw/Morley%20South%20ward%20by-election%20-%2012%20June%202025.pdf) gives six candidate totals summing to 5,755 and 13 rejected ballots. The [secondary summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Leeds_City_Council_election#Changes_between_2024_and_2026) reports 31.7% turnout, but its stated ballot count is one below 5,755 + 13. Candidate votes remain **official**; v0.5.0 displays rounded turnout as **approximate secondary data**, labelled on the contest and history. | Obtain official turnout/electorate or resolve the one-ballot discrepancy. |
| 2021 Kirkstall | Council CSV lists the Conservative candidate on 733; the Commons-derived release and [2021 Handbook PDF](https://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/LEH2021-complete.pdf) list 773. The release uses 773 and visibly notes the conflict. | Council correction or written explanation; otherwise retain the Handbook-based decision and an explicit unresolved flag. |
| 2023 Calverley & Farsley; Headingley & Hyde Park | Twelve council spreadsheet candidate fields are zero while Commons records and the [2023 Handbook PDF](https://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/LEH-2023-Complete.pdf) have non-zero results. The release uses the Handbook values and notes both contests. | Corrected council rows or written clarification; verify each candidate and total. |
| 2024 Roundhay | Two council spreadsheet fields are one vote lower than Commons and the [2024 Handbook PDF](https://www.electionscentre.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/LEH2024-Complete.pdf): Lisa Martin 4,039/4,040 and Shazar Ahad 839/840. The release uses the Handbook values and notes the contest. | Corrected declaration or council explanation for both votes. |
| By-election register | Official archive checks found the Farnley 2024 and Morley South 2025 by-elections, and the package records a future Calverley & Farsley poll announced for 22 October 2026. This is not proof that every relevant by-election was found. “Latest recorded” is deliberately narrower than “current seat holder.” | Reconcile a council-provided event list or other complete official register against all 33 ward histories for the covered period; document exclusions and election dates. |

The 15 candidate differences are one in Kirkstall, twelve across the two 2023 wards and two in Roundhay. The detailed comparison and its limits are in [the 25 September results audit](leeds-release-audit-2026-09-25.md). The council archive and Handbook sources disagree; favouring the corroborated Handbook values is an explicit publication choice, not proof that the council files were corrected. Exact byte comparison of the four local Commons workbooks with fresh upstream downloads is also outstanding.

Party switches change composition without a poll. The [2024–26 secondary event list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Leeds_City_Council_election#Defections_and_resignations) notes Trish Smith's Conservative → Independent → Reform switch, Labour-to-Independent departures by Abigail Marshall Katung and Zara Hussain, and the Sewards and Kidger resignations leading to the two by-elections. The [2026–27 secondary event list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Leeds_City_Council_election#Changes_between_2026_and_2027) notes Jessica Lennox and Cristiana Mirosanu becoming independent and Peter Carlill resigning. [Leeds City Council's May 2026 count](https://news.leeds.gov.uk/news/new-political-make-up-of-leeds-city-council-5) included three additional vacancy seats in that scheduled election. The current Composition tab uses the separately dated [council 99-seat snapshot](https://www.leeds.gov.uk/councillors-and-democracy/councillors-and-committees); it must not be inferred from latest ward winners.

Before any corrected package goes live: capture the new source and its hash; rerun the crosscheck and package validators; inspect each changed ward, SDP summary and composition view; record approval with a reason; then activate the immutable release. An unresolved issue can remain in a trial if it is visible and excluded from calculations that need it.
