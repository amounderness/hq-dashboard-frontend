The [Leeds City Council results archive](https://datamillnorth.org/dataset/local-election-results-20jwj), linked from its [election results page](https://www.leeds.gov.uk/elections/election-results), was reviewed across the covered 2021–2026 period. It lists regular election result files for 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2026, plus two stand-alone by-election declarations: Farnley & Wortley on 10 October 2024 and Morley South on 12 June 2025. Both are represented in v0.6.0. The October 2024 Farnley declaration is blank, so its recorded candidate tally and turnout remain clearly attributed to [West Leeds Dispatch](https://westleedsdispatch.com/farnley-wortley-by-election-result-green-partys-david-blackburn-retakes-seat/). Morley South's candidate tally uses the [council declaration](https://datamillnorth.org/download/20jwj/bbw/Morley%20South%20ward%20by-election%20-%2012%20June%202025.pdf), and its turnout remains an approximate secondary figure. The 22 October 2026 Calverley & Farsley by-election is [announced but has not happened](https://www.leeds.gov.uk/elections/leeds-city-council-elections) at this check date.

The council has [33 wards with three councillors each](https://www.leeds.gov.uk/councillors-and-democracy/councillors-and-committees). An ordinary poll usually fills one of the ward's three positions; an additional vacancy can mean two positions are filled together. The package validation checks seat counts for each recorded poll, including extra seats in [2021](https://news.leeds.gov.uk/news/new-political-make-up-of-leeds-city-council-1), [2022](https://news.leeds.gov.uk/news/new-political-make-up-of-leeds-city-council-2) and [2026](https://news.leeds.gov.uk/news/new-political-make-up-of-leeds-city-council-5). Poll results now display “1 of 3” or “2 of 3” ward seats. They do **not** identify every councillor serving after the poll.

The archive check closes the defined event-list reconciliation for this pilot. It is not a claim that a full person-by-person register, every subsequent defection, or every date of tenure has been certified. A third-party index appeared to count Farnley & Wortley twice on different October 2024 dates; the council archive contains a single 10 October declaration, so no second poll was added.

For council composition, the [8 May 2026 council announcement](https://news.leeds.gov.uk/news/new-political-make-up-of-leeds-city-council-5) recorded Labour 48 and Green 11. The [council composition page](https://www.leeds.gov.uk/councillors-and-democracy/councillors-and-committees), captured 26 September, recorded Labour 46, Green 10, Independent 3 and one vacancy. The latest Composition view compares those **two saved dates**; it does not call the difference an election result. Its 2026 election view compares with the previous saved May 2024 snapshot, explicitly spanning two years. Reported intervening changes are noted from a [secondary timeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Leeds_City_Council_election#Changes_between_2026_and_2027) and should not be read as a certified complete councillor register.

Release checks: all 36 manifest-hashed data objects passed the package validator; targeted assertions checked each selected candidate figure, preserved 2023 Handbook tallies, three-seat ward labels, event coverage, share denominators and the composition snapshots. The immutable package is stored privately in R2 and requires owner validation and approval before activation.
