## Result gaps uncovered while checking the schedules

The local rc4 store has only an **upcoming** Leeds Calverley and Farsley event for 22 October 2026; it has no completed 2026 by-election result. [Leeds's council page](https://www.leeds.gov.uk/elections/leeds-city-council-elections) has the notice and candidates, but there is no result yet as of this review date. Keep it labelled upcoming.

[Sheffield City Council's result index](https://www.sheffield.gov.uk/your-city-council/elections/city-council-parish-council-election-results) lists two completed 2026 city council by-elections absent from rc4: **Southey, 27 August**, and **Walkley, 17 September**. The [signed Southey declaration](https://www.sheffield.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-08/declaration-of-result-southey-by-election-27-august-2026.pdf) reports Paul Jakeman (Reform UK) elected with 1,133 votes, five candidates, 2,243 ballots issued, three rejected, 14,348 electors and 15.63% turnout. The [Walkley declaration](https://www.sheffield.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2026-09/declaration_of_result_-_walkley_by-election_17_september_2026.pdf) reports Hannah Cawley (Labour and Co-operative Party) elected with 2,315 votes, six candidates, 4,340 ballots issued, eight rejected, 15,401 electors and 28.18% turnout. **Source discrepancy:** the council's aggregated results index gives Reform UK's Andrew Telford 258 votes, but the individual Walkley page and declaration give **541**. The declaration's candidate votes sum to 4,332, exactly 4,340 issued minus eight rejected; the index's 258 leaves a 283-vote gap. Use 541 with an explicit source-disagreement note when importing. These are **source findings, not yet imported results**. Verify ward IDs and event-date geography before a new, immutable release candidate. The index is not assumed exhaustive for other councils.

## Next engineering decision

The current council by-election importer is intentionally fixed to 2025. Before extending it to 2026 and other areas, review a generic dated-event import contract that keeps council declarations, transcription hashes, turnout discrepancies and coverage updates separate from the annual-results import. That contract and its tests can then support a new release ID without rewriting rc4. No schema or importer refactor is included in this schedule-review change.
